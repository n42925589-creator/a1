About Rainwear Bronze

An independent guide to rain jackets, created for walkers, commuters, cyclists and anyone who would rather enjoy wet weather than hide from it.

Our story

Born on a very wet coastal walk

Rainwear Bronze started after a coastal walk where every jacket in the group behaved differently. One stayed dry, one felt clammy, and one soaked through at the shoulders within an hour. Nobody could explain why.

Finding the answers meant learning about membranes, seam tape, ratings and care. We built this website to share that knowledge clearly, without jargon or sales pressure.

Independent

We do not sell jackets and we are not affiliated with any brand, manufacturer or retailer.

Practical

Our guides focus on real decisions: what to look for, how to fit it and how to make it last.

Long-lasting

We encourage caring for and repairing rainwear so it stays in use for many seasons.

How we work

Research you can trust

Our guides draw on textile references, published fabric test standards, care-labelling guidance and the experience of hikers, cyclists and everyday commuters. We explain how fabrics and features generally perform rather than ranking products.
